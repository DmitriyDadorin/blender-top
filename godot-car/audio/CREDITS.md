# Audio sources

- Engine idle/load and ignition: **lonemonk**, “Chrysler Start and Idle (Exhaust perspective)”, **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**. https://freesound.org/people/lonemonk/sounds/240671/ — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . A real 1964 Chrysler Windsor exhaust recording; `source/chrysler_v8_exhaust.mp3` is the author's high-quality preview, downloaded from https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/240/240671_230160-hq.mp3 . Adaptations: excerpts (12–20 seconds for both loops; 0.65–2.8 seconds for ignition), EQ, normalization, loop crossfades, runtime pitch and gain changes.
- Horn and handbrake recordings: **GGBotNet**, “Car Sound Effects Pack (Low Quality)”, **CC0**. https://opengameart.org/content/car-sound-effects-pack-low-quality . The earlier engine OGG recordings remain in the source archive but are no longer used or processed into the game.
- City traffic recording: **IgnasD**, “High traffic road sounds”, **CC0**. https://opengameart.org/content/high-traffic-road-sounds
- Coastal waves recording: **jasinski**, submitted by qubodup, “Beach Ocean Waves”, **CC0**. https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves
- Tyre squeal: **audible-edge (Tom Haigh)**, submitted by qubodup, “Car tire squeal skid loop”, **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**. https://opengameart.org/content/car-tire-squeal-skid-loop — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Recordings were resampled to 44.1 kHz, equalized, level-normalized and crossfaded for seamless looping; tyre playback rate and gain change with driving. Original downloads are in `source/`; processing is reproducible with `tools/prepare_audio.py`. Road roll and wind are original filtered-noise beds generated by that script.

The engine uses matching excerpts from a new exhaust recording, with bass emphasis and a softened upper midrange. The two layers share loop duration and playback rate to preserve one coherent note; the load layer becomes stronger under throttle. A separate in-game engine bus filters the result after pitch changes.
