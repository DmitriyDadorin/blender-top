Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished startup splash artwork for a small coastal driving video game. Create a polished original landscape illustration, 4:3 aspect ratio, ideally 1536x1152 pixels. Retro 1980s California travel-poster and album-cover feeling, crisp graphic shapes with subtle painterly finish, luminous sunset, elegant and uncluttered. A teal-blue angular classic sports coupe seen from behind in the lower center drives along a dark asphalt coastal boulevard toward the horizon. Pacific ocean and a small yacht on the left, palms framing the sides, pastel modern pool villas and a restrained distant city skyline on the right. Coral-pink and peach horizon, deep violet upper sky, dark indigo road, turquoise sea, warm cream highlights. Strong readable composition at small screen sizes, generous sky area. In the upper central sky place one large beautifully designed cream display wordmark reading EXACTLY 'PALMSHIFT', spelled P A L M S H I F T. Distinctive bold slightly forward-slanted lettering, carefully spaced, not stretched, no other text. Keep the wordmark and main car within the central 80% safe area. Full bleed illustration, no border, no UI, no engine logo, no developer logo, no watermark, no extra words. The car and world should look stylized and approachable rather than photorealistic; realistic perspective and coherent road geometry.
